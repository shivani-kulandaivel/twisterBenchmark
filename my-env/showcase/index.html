★ HOW TO PLAY TWISTER ★

Twister is a game of physical contortion played on a vinyl mat dotted with big colored circles.

red yellow green blue

A spinner calls out a move pairing one limb with one color, e.g. "right hand → green". You must place that hand or foot onto a free circle of that color…

…without falling and without lifting your other planted limbs. Each new call leaves you more tangled. Touch the mat with a knee or elbow, or topple over, and you're OUT. Last one standing wins!

ROBOT MODE: an AI drives a physics-simulated humanoid's joints to land the called limb(s) on the right color. We score the placement error (distance to target) and whether it stays upright.
